Mission Statement

Nightshade-25 is a next-generation thermal cloaking system engineered to provide short-term infrared concealment in high-risk combat zones. Designed with front-line operators and drone teams in mind, it uses affordable, mass-producible phase-change materials (PCMs) and infrared-diffusing composites to mask heat sources — from human bodies to electronics — during critical exposure windows.

The system is entirely passive and recharges without power, using ambient night air to reset its thermal masking capability. Nightshade-25 offers a field-ready balance of effectiveness, simplicity, and scalability — bringing advanced thermal stealth within reach of decentralized and rapidly deployed forces.

Key Features

  • Thermal signature suppression for 2–4 hours — masks human bodies, vehicles, and machinery from thermal optics
  • Low-cost and mass-producible — constructed from abundant, non-toxic materials using basic manufacturing methods
  • Passively rechargeable — resets using ambient night air; no power supply or electronics required
  • Lightweight, modular tile system — deploy as wraps, blankets, or panels; 3–6 kg per 1.5 m² cloak
  • Effective against FLIR and drone-mounted thermal imagers — built for real-world battlefield IR systems
  • Real-world tested performance — validated under IR in outdoor field conditions with consistent stealth duration
  • Multi-layer design — combines thermally absorbing and IR-reflecting layers to maximize effectiveness
